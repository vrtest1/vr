Surface detail: GLOBAL 4K
OBS frames are generated in-browser from the EUMETSAT EUMETView global composite. GLOBAL uses the IR 10.8 µm nominal time as its reference; Dust RGB and Natural Colour RGB are used only when the same nominal time is advertised. If the latest EUMETSAT composite is behind real time, 3-hour HYBRID frames are generated up to the latest completed 3-hour slot. They extrapolate motion from the two newest OBS cloud-alpha frames and use NOAA GFS total-cloud-cover guidance obtained through the Open-Meteo API. HYBRID is a forecast-assisted estimate, not a satellite observation. The surface uses NASA Blue Marble and city lights use NASA Black Marble. All displayed frame times use this device’s local time zone. Sun position, day/night shading, and city lights are synchronized to the selected frame time. Manual frame selection and TIME LAPSE use the same frame-rendering path. Drag: rotate / Wheel or pinch: zoom. SUN OFF disables solar day/night shading and lights the whole globe uniformly for easier viewing. v0.15 keeps the adaptive drag behavior and adds regional NASA Blue Marble surface LOD. When you zoom in, a higher-resolution WMS patch is loaded only around the current view center, with soft feathered edges and a small LRU texture cache. SUN OFF remains a uniform full-globe viewing mode. In XR, Grip lets you hold, move, and freely tilt the globe with your hand. The right stick rotates around Earth's own north-south axis. Quest buttons: A CLOUDS, B SUN, X CITY LIGHTS, Y INITIAL VIEW. TIME LAPSE keeps whatever state it had before entering AR/VR.
